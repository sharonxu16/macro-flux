> [!abstract] Overview
> Oil surged roughly 5% after President Trump made sweeping new demands on Iran, dashing hopes for a quick Strait of Hormuz deal and reviving energy‑inflation fears that pushed Treasury yields and the dollar higher while equities pulled back from records. Gold broke above `$4,400/oz` for the first time in two months as traders sought safety ahead of this week’s US CPI release. The session’s cross‑market thread was a split between energy‑driven inflation jitters and idiosyncratic Asia FX optimism, leaving the dollar caught between higher US yields and renminbi‑led EM resilience.
