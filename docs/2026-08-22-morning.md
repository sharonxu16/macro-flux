> [!abstract] Overview
> Bessent’s Treasury-market push is backfiring rather than calming markets: long-end yields kept rising, breakevens hit the highest in more than two months, and the dollar slid to a three-month low while Bitcoin and gold surged on explicit debasement trades. Washington’s fiscal credibility is now the dominant global macro driver, feeding inflation expectations and hard-asset demand. In China, July fiscal revenue growth accelerated to `11.7%` with individual income tax up `25.9%`, and the finance ministry signalled more incremental policies for the second half. On the trade front, China’s surplus against the European Union widened in July across all but three members as the October deadline approaches, while the US-China AI-bloc contest tightened in Southeast Asia. The cross-market thread is fiscal credibility: ineffective US yield control is forcing investors into gold, Bitcoin, and non-dollar exposure, while Beijing leans on fiscal expansion to stabilize domestic demand.
