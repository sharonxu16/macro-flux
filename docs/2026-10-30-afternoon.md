> [!abstract] Overview
> Asia afternoon trade was dominated by a landmark BOJ inflation declaration colliding with a deepening AI-led tech selloff: Tokyo equities slid as much as `3%` and Singapore-listed AEM dropped `11%`, while the yen made only a brief gain before broad risk-off flows resumed. The BOJ held rates but declared its `2%` inflation goal finally reached, signaling further hikes this year and keeping pressure on global bond yields even as AI jitters drag equities. That defensive tone was amplified by escalating energy war risk, with **Iran reporting four tankers struck by mines in the Strait of Hormuz** and Ukraine hitting a Russian oil refinery, lifting oil and energy-importing Asia FX vulnerability. The cross-market thread is a clash between reflation-driven tightening signals and a tech and energy risk-off, leaving oil and Asia FX as the main pressure valves.
