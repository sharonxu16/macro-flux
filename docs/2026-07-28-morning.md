### 📌 Global chip rout triggers 6% KOSPI crash, SK Hynix slumps; AI spending doubts spread

**Fact**
A selloff in chipmakers deepened as mounting doubts over returns from billions of dollars of artificial‑intelligence spending rippled through semiconductor stocks from Wall Street to Asia ([BBG](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/stock-market-today-dow-s-p-live-updates)). The KOSPI index fell 6%, triggering circuit breakers and falling below 6,200 to its lowest since April ([ChnFund_Macro](https://www.chnfund.com/article/ARd4fc3dc6-2efb-1491-6bd4-3a22b8a5fe98)). SK Hynix Inc.’s American depositary receipts slumped to a new low after its record‑setting `$26.5 billion` debut earlier this month ([BBG](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/sk-hynix-slumps-below-us-ipo-price-as-mega-listings-turn-sour)). Shares in CXMT soared `466%` on its Shanghai Star Market debut, making it China’s most valuable listed company, but the broader chip sector tumbled ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/cxmt-china-market-debut-chipmaker-ipo.html)).

> [!info] [AI Reasoning]
> * **Narrative change**: New — AI capex fatigue sparks an acute unwind in semiconductor‑heavy Asian markets, with Korea at the epicenter.
> * **Transmission / Market Read**: Capital‑flow channel — supports **short KRW**, **short TWD**, and **long USD/Asia**; equity‑safe‑haven rotation favors **long UST** and **long JPY**.
> * **Watchpoint / Confidence**: Stabilisation of KOSPI outflows or chip earnings guidance (Nvidia, TSMC) that can halt the selloff. Confidence: High given the breadth of the selloff.
