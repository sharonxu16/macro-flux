> [!abstract] Overview
> The Treasury buyback-led bond rally lost intraday steam as yields edged higher and analysts warned the intervention may be only a short-lived circuit breaker, leaving the dollar near a three-month low and gold off a two-month peak. Oil pushed to a three-week high after Washington formalized an Economic D-Day against Iran and threatened secondary sanctions on any country doing business with Tehran, while tanker demand jumped even as Hormuz shipping data stayed unchanged. In Asia, Pyongyang answered a US olive branch with a missile launch and Taiwan proposed a record 2027 defense budget, adding a regional security premium. China rolled out a fresh round of Shanghai property easing while its government bonds continued to defy the global yield surge. The cross-market thread is a tension between liquidity-friendly Treasury intervention and resurgent energy and geopolitical risk premia, with China’s domestic easing providing a partial offset.
