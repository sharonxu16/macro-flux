> [!abstract] Overview
> The dollar's broad surge, led by a euro slump to a 17-month low on French debt and political risks, dominated afternoon price action while Treasury yields eased as investors further pared Federal Reserve rate-hike bets. Oil faded on signs Middle East crude exports have recovered above pre-war levels and on the G-7 stock release, though tanker attacks are rising and Saudi Aramco's chief warned global inventories are dangerously thin and could take up to two years to rebuild. The contradictory energy picture kept Brent supported above the century mark even as physical headlines improved, while gold and copper gained on reduced tightening risk. US-China security friction intensified with an alleged Chinese agent arrested in California for surveilling the Taiwanese president's son and US defense experts reportedly set to work inside Taiwan's military headquarters. The cross-market thread is a strengthening dollar against fragile European fiscal and Asian currencies, while softer US rate expectations battle persistent energy supply tail risk and Sino-American escalation.
