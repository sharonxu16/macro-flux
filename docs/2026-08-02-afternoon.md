> [!abstract] Overview
> A sharp intraday de-escalation remade the geopolitical map after President Trump held off on new strikes against Iran, claiming the “perimeters of a deal” had been agreed to, unleashing a powerful retracement in energy and a flight into risk assets. Simultaneously, the United States’ historic intervention deepened into a coordinated tripartite force with Japan and Korea, converging into a massive defense of Asian currencies. In China, the PBOC explicitly pivoted to a pro-growth stance, vowing to adjust policy in a timely manner and forcefully expand domestic demand. These three forces combine to install a temporary ceiling on oil volatility, a strong put under Asia FX led by the yen and won, and broad support for equities in a climate where a sudden Middle East peace bid is upending every defensive trade laid on this morning.
