> [!abstract] Overview
> The dominant macro driver is the global government bond selloff: the 10-year US Treasury yield hit its highest since 2007, global bond yields approached 4%, and US real yields moved near levels last seen around the Lehman crisis. That repricing is already transmitting into emerging-market bond outflows, a technical breakdown in gold, and softer US equities even as oil spiked and then pulled back on Middle East uncertainty. US-China trade implementation also looks bumpy, with soybean farmers excluded from tariff relief and Trump contradicting his ambassador on weapons sales to China, while Beijing presses a new AI and frontier-tech industrial policy push. The cross-market thread is higher long-end real rates compressing duration, gold, EM debt, and equities until central banks or data break the repricing.
