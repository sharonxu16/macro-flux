> [!abstract] Overview
> Risk assets fell overnight as the Federal Reserve minutes confirmed unanimous September tightening and signaled another hike this year, while the New York Fed survey showed one-year inflation expectations at `3.9%`, the highest since May 2023. US yields pushed long-dated Treasuries toward multidecade highs, the dollar traded near an 18-month high, and equities slipped. Oil whipsawed on fresh Hormuz escalation after the White House requested strike options and weekly tanker attacks hit the highest since the war started, though supply optimism later tempered gains. Europe’s fiscal stress persisted despite official pushback, and China-EU trade relations hardened with Beijing rejecting EU hybrid car export curb requests. The connecting thread is a tightening dollar-liquidity and energy-risk premium shock that pressures EM carry and Asian currencies.
