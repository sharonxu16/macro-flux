> [!abstract] Overview
> Oil supply risks escalated overnight after a Saudi East-West pipeline attack shut a critical bypass route, pushing crude to `$109` and reinforcing an inflation shock that is now dominating the global rates complex. The 10-year Treasury yield traded through `5%` for the first time since 2023, the dollar strengthened, and markets moved to a greater than `92%` probability that the Fed raises this week with another hike favored in December. Equity markets were caught between AI slowdown warnings and higher yields, with US chip names and Asian semiconductor shares sliding while gold declined. The main cross-market thread is an energy-driven hawkish repricing: higher crude is feeding inflation expectations, pulling up US yields and the dollar, and pressuring rate-sensitive and AI-led equity valuations.
