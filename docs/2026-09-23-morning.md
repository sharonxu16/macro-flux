> [!abstract] Overview
> Oil led the tape lower as Iran signaled conditional readiness to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and Saudi Arabia moved to restart a key East-West pipeline, with Brent sliding below `$100` and bearish wagers surging to unprecedented levels. The energy relief collided with a still-hawkish Fed message: Barkin warned one hike may not be enough and inflation is broadening beyond energy and tariff shocks, even as rates traders loaded hedges for a shallower cycle. Equities extended the AI rally, the Nasdaq 100 closing at its first record since June and Asian stocks rising for a sixth day, while financials fell on AI-disruption fears and a flattening curve. Into Thursday’s Xi-Trump summit, Beijing tightened fentanyl precursor controls, state media framed the aim as constructive strategic stability, and the two sides reportedly set up an AI dialogue after Taiwan frictions. The cross-market thread is energy relief cooling inflation pressure, supporting duration and tech, while Fed hawkishness and summit tail risks cut both ways.
