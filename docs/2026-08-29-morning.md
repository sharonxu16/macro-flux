> Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh used Jackson Hole to shift the policy debate from patient disinflation watch to an explicit near-term hike contingency, driving front-end Treasury yields sharply higher, lifting the dollar, and knocking equities and gold lower.

> The cross-market thread is renewed central bank hawkishness colliding with an unsettled global oil supply map, keeping the dollar supported and risk assets on the defensive.
