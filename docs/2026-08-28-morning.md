> [!abstract] Overview
> Markets are on hold into Kevin Warsh’s Jackson Hole address after three Fed officials warned policy may not be restrictive enough, leaving US stocks, bonds and gold little changed. ### 📌 Bank of Korea delivers second straight hike

**Fact**
The Bank of Korea (BOK) lifted its benchmark rate by `0.25 percentage point` to `3.0 per cent` on Thursday, a month after an identical increase took it to `2.75 per cent` in July ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/week-asia/economics/article/3365480/strong-signal-south-korea-raises-rates-again-fight-inflation-rising-home-prices)). The latest move brought the rate to its highest level since February 2025 and signalled a central bank willing to accept some damage to domestic demand to contain broader financial risks ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/week-asia/economics/article/3365480/strong-signal-south-korea-raises-rates-again-fight-inflation-rising-home-prices)). South Korea’s central bank has raised interest rates for a second straight month, with analysts saying its semiconductor boom has given policymakers room to confront inflation, a weak won and rising home prices, but at the cost of a deeper squeeze on indebted households and small businesses ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/week-asia/economics/article/3365480/strong-signal-south-korea-raises-rates-again-fight-inflation-rising-home-prices)).

> [!info] [AI Reasoning]
> * **Narrative change**: Confirmation of BOK tightening cycle; second hike concretizes priority on inflation and housing.
> * **Transmission / Market Read**: Policy and capital-flow channel favors **long KRW** and Korean front-end, tempered by domestic demand drag.
> * **Watchpoint / Confidence**: Further BOK communication and US data direction; Confidence: High given official action.
