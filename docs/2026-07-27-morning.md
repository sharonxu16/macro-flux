> [!abstract] Overview
> Oil tumbled and bonds rallied after the US paused military strikes on Iran over the weekend, easing immediate supply‑disruption fears even as Houthi rebels claimed attacks on Saudi Red Sea oil sites and the US pressed Oman‑mediated talks on Strait of Hormuz management. The reprieve came hours after the US triggered a second shock: sweeping new tariffs on 60 trading partners including seven ASEAN nations, drawing an immediate legal challenge and threats of retaliation from Beijing, Brasília, and beyond. The combination of easing Mid‑East war risk and an escalating trade war leaves the Federal Reserve’s rate decision this week finely balanced, with markets pricing roughly a one‑in‑three chance of a hike. Singapore’s MAS policy statement later this morning and FOMC guidance on oil‑driven inflation are the twin catalysts that will shape Asian FX and rates this week.
