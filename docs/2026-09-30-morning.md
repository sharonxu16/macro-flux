> [!abstract] Overview
> China delivered a coordinated easing push overnight, cutting the PSL rate by `25bp` and expanding structural relending while rolling out first-ever central government mortgage interest subsidies for first homes, reinforcing growth support ahead of the holiday. The US long end re-priced to a multi-decade extreme, with the `30-year Treasury yield` touching its highest since 2002 even as Fed officials signaled a further hike this year but not necessarily in October, and September consumer confidence fell on elevated oil prices and inflation worries. In the Gulf, a KOTC tanker was struck in the Strait of Hormuz, bunker prices escalated, and Washington moved to release up to `40 million barrels` from the Strategic Petroleum Reserve. The cross-market thread: easier China liquidity collides with a global term-premium shock and renewed Hormuz supply risk, keeping inflation, oil, and USD funding costs as the dominant constraints on Asia FX.
