> [!abstract] Overview
> Oil crashed as much as 8% after President Trump announced a pause in US strikes on Iran and the two sides began talks to end the conflict, deflating the supply-risk premium that had pushed Brent above `$100`. Singapore’s central bank delivered a surprise monetary policy tightening in a preemptive move against oil-driven imported inflation, jolting Asian FX markets. The cross-market thread is an unwind of the Iran war premium — commodities down, yields down, tech risk-off via a chip stock rout — that challenges the hawkish central-bank repricing just as the FOMC looms.

### 📌 Singapore MAS Surprises with Tightening as Oil Stirs Inflation Fears

**Fact**
Singapore tightened monetary policy in a surprise move as rising oil prices rekindle inflation risk ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/singapore-mas-july-monetary-policy-middle-east-oil-prices-inflation-.html)). The move represents a preemptive strike against second-round inflation effects before they fully materialize in domestic price data ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/singapore-mas-july-monetary-policy-middle-east-oil-prices-inflation-.html)). The policy shift reinforces the SGD nominal effective exchange rate as a tool to anchor imported inflation when global energy markets are volatile ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/singapore-mas-july-monetary-policy-middle-east-oil-prices-inflation-.html)).

> [!info] [AI Reasoning]
> * **Narrative change**: New — an unscheduled policy move from an Asia-Pacific central bank explicitly tied to oil-driven inflation risk creates a direct monetary transmission channel from the Iran conflict to regional FX.
> * **Transmission / Market Read**: Policy-tightening channel tilts risks toward **stronger SGD** versus regional baskets and raises the probability that other Asian central banks with oil-import exposure follow with tightening or hawkish holds.
> * **Watchpoint / Confidence**: MAS statement language on future slope adjustments; confirmation if BOK or BSP signal similar preemptive tightening at their next meetings. Confidence: High — the move is explicit and cross-source confirmed.
