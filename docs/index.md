> [!abstract] Overview
> Global risk appetite enters Monday under heavy strain after US tech equities plunged into a bear market in the worst week since Covid, with the AI trade unwind dragging the Nasdaq 100 toward bear-market territory and forcing Asia’s largest markets into emergency support. Beijing has cut equity margin requirements to `80%` and South Korea has rolled out phased market stabilization measures, while early US equity futures are attempting a bounce and Treasury futures are lower. Separate from equities, Middle East escalation is tightening physical oil risk: Bahrain reports an Iranian projectile hit an industrial facility, Yemen’s Houthis claim an attack on Saudi Arabia’s Rabigh refinery, and Hormuz crude flows have fallen even as the UAE shuttles cargoes past Iran. US-China tensions remain unresolved around the FCC ban on Chinese telecoms, Nvidia chip sales, and Taiwan arms discussions, adding a policy-risk layer to the already fragile tech complex. Treasury Secretary Bessent’s warning that the Fed risks overtightening adds a rates-policy dimension to the equity drawdown. The unifying thread is a simultaneous risk reduction across AI/tech, energy, and US-China policy that keeps volatility elevated and Asia FX defensive into the new week.
