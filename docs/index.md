## 🔬 Narrative Watch

### 📌 Hot US CPI accelerates Fed repricing; Waller opens the door to 50bp

**Fact**
The consumer price index rose `0.4%` in October, more than expected ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/cpi-inflation-report-october-2026.html)). US core CPI rose by more than forecast `0.3%`, supporting a rate hike ([BBG](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/us-core-cpi-rises-more-than-forecast-0-3-supporting-rate-hike)). Energy inflation largely dropped out of the headline rate in October and added to worries of inflation persistence ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/4f3334ce-3307-4b9a-bcf8-ba69ca6b8f80)). Fed Governor Christopher Waller says policymakers should weigh a `50bp` move if US inflation fails to slow ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/72abed21-6043-4b2b-81b1-f3a2547da5b7)); he sees a December hike as likely and opens the door to a possible half-point increase ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/fed-governor-waller-sees-december-hike-likely.html)). Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan said she still sees the need for at least two quarter-point rate increases, but that the pace of those hikes may need to be quicker than the Fed is moving this fall ([BBG](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/fed-s-logan-favors-at-least-two-hikes-says-pace-could-quicken)). The 10-year Treasury yield raced back to `5%` as inflation data and Waller comments drove rate-hike expectations ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/us-bonds-treasurys-yields.html)). The dollar rallied the most since September after hotter-than-forecast US inflation reinforced expectations for a Federal Reserve interest-rate hike next month ([BBG](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/dollar-set-for-best-day-since-september-as-cpi-lifts-hike-bets)). Stocks ended lower as rate-hike odds climbed after hot inflation ([BBG](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/us-stock-futures-trade-mixed-with-focus-on-cpi-anthropic-ipo)). Gold held losses near `$4,000` on hot US inflation and Fed remarks ([BBG](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/gold-holds-loss-near-4-000-on-hot-us-inflation-fed-remarks)).
