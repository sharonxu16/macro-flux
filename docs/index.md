> [!abstract] Overview
> The dominant macro driver entering the new week is the unresolved Iran conflict. Qatari-mediated talks entered a third day and President Trump said the war would be "wrapped up soon," yet fresh attacks near the Strait of Hormuz, including on a Pakistan LNG carrier that had Iranian approval, kept physical oil risk alive even as crude slipped into the US midterm. That geopolitical layer sits on top of a deepening AI trade unwind, with US tech shares extending Friday's rout and Fed officials flagging at least two more rate hikes, which pushed gold to a one-year low and silver below `$50`. China provided the main Asian offset. The PBOC formally switched its policy rate anchor from the seven-day reverse repo to the overnight rate at a level below the old benchmark, while settlement inflows drove onshore and offshore RMB through `6.66` and `6.65`. Reports of a PBOC-CSRC meeting to study stock market stabilization add support, even as October's official manufacturing PMI slipped to `49.3`. The cross-market thread is a hawkish Fed, a fragile US risk complex, and China-specific easing plus currency strength pulling Asia FX in opposite directions.
