> [!abstract] Overview
> Risk sentiment, not inflation, drove the afternoon tape: the dollar pulled back from an 18-month high and Treasury yields fell as the AI sell-off deepened, with Nasdaq futures down roughly `1%` and global chipmakers under pressure. That unwound part of the previous session’s hot-CPI hawkish repricing even though September CPI had accelerated, leaving Fed hike bets below their highs ahead of tonight’s US PPI, retail sales, and jobless claims. Oil slipped despite two more cargo ships sunk in the Strait of Hormuz, as the market weighed a possible US easing of shadow-fleet sanctions to relieve freight rates. China’s yuan faced fresh IMF pushback on stimulus ambition, while Singapore’s `5.4%` third-quarter GDP showed regional resilience. The common thread is a risk-off rotation out of AI equities and into safe havens, with commodity and Asia FX paths hinging on tonight’s US data and any further Hormuz escalation.
