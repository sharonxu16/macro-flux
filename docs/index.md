> [!abstract] Overview
> Risk assets fell into the weekend as Brent pushed to `$117` and Iran’s reported plans to target Gulf undersea cables added a fresh supply-chain and risk-premium shock. That oil-driven inflation impulse collided with an accelerating AI de-rating, where chipmakers logged their longest weekly drop since November and havens behaved unusually, with gold falling below `$4,000` even as investors bought US Treasuries. US consumer sentiment slipped to a five-month low on inflation pressures, reinforcing the stagflationary mix of costly energy and cooling demand. France received a Moody’s reprieve at `Aa3`, but euro-area sovereign stress remains a live risk around high borrowing costs and uneven core yields. The common thread is an inflation-risk premium from energy geopolitics now feeding into a growth-valuation unwind and complicating central bank repricing across DM economies.
