> [!abstract] Overview
> A sharp AI capital-spending repricing hit global equities after Meta guided to hundreds of billions of dollars in future spending, dragging Nasdaq lower, lifting Treasuries, and setting Asian stocks up for a defensive open. The equity shock is colliding with an oil supply shock as Brent broke above `$113` on renewed Saudi attacks and US-Iran strike risks, while France’s sovereign stress reached crisis levels and pulled the US 10-year yield back below `5%`. Japan’s core inflation printed `2.1%` and US initial jobless claims fell to `194,000`, keeping developed-market labor markets tight and Asia central-bank repricing alive. The cross-market thread is a global flight to safety that rewards US duration, yen and dollar liquidity, while punishing AI-heavy equities and oil-sensitive Asia FX.
