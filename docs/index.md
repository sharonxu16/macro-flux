> [!abstract] Overview
> The sudden pivot from military strikes to diplomacy on Iran sent oil tumbling and risk appetite recovering as President Trump announced talks beginning Monday covering the Strait of Hormuz and denuclearization. In currencies, the historic US-Japan coordinated yen intervention deepened, with the US Treasury selling euros to buy yen, driving the currency sharply higher and crushing yen shorts. Meanwhile, hawkish Fed dissent hardened after three rate-setters voted to hike, Chair Warsh floated a rethink of meeting frequency, and eurozone inflation accelerated to 2.9%, keeping global rate-hike pressure on the front burner. China’s PBOC pledged timely policy adjustments to support the economy, and Hong Kong flagged an upgrade to its full-year GDP forecast after 5.1% first-half growth. The collision of geopolitical de-escalation and persistent central bank hawkishness leaves this week’s US jobs data as the decisive catalyst for both rates and Asia FX direction.
