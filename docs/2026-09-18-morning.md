> [!abstract] Overview
> Markets consolidated the Fed’s first hike since 2023. Treasury yields slipped, the dollar paused its six-day advance, and US equities rebounded as investors chose to read Chair Warsh’s tightening as a confidence signal rather than a policy error. The Bank of England held at `3.75%` but shifted more hawkish, warning it may need to hike if the Middle East conflict keeps inflation elevated, while overhauling gilt sales to stop long-dated QT, which supported UK bonds. Attention now turns to Friday’s BOJ decision with the yen pressing `156` and strategists positioned for a dovish disappointment. Oil cooled for a third session as Saudi Arabia rerouted crude sales outside Hormuz after the East-West pipeline shutdown, while Washington’s approval of tariffs on buyers of Russian energy sharpens the China risk ahead of Xi’s visit. The cross-market thread is a fragile easing in rates and oil-led risk sentiment that depends on BOJ guidance and the next Iran-war headline.
