> [!abstract] Overview
> Middle East energy risk and a global bond sell-off are the two dominant macro forces. Houthi claims of a strike on an Aramco facility, a US aircraft carrier buildup, and continued crude transit through the Strait of Hormuz keep a geopolitical risk premium on oil. At the same time, Fed and ECB hike expectations have faded after soft US jobs data and acute French market stress, while Treasury officials downplay yields at multi-decade highs. Asian security headlines add tension: North Korea tested an intermediate-range missile and the US is deploying an anti-ship system to Yonaguni, near Taiwan. The common thread is a risk premium on energy and rates, with Asian FX caught between commodity pressure and regional security.
