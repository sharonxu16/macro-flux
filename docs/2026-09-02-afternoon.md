> [!abstract] Overview
> The Iran-driven reflation shock extended intraday: the dollar climbed to a two-week high and gold fell to its lowest in more than three weeks as the Hormuz conflict entered a tanker-for-tanker phase, with Washington striking Iranian mine capability and Tehran hitting Kuwait, Bahrain and Jordan. BOJ hawks explicitly signaled a possible September hike and back-to-back moves, deepening the JGB rout and global capital rotation. Korea’s FX authorities bought `$20 billion` of repatriated SK Hynix dollars to stabilize the won, a direct defense against oil-driven risk-off. The cross-market thread is an oil supply shock feeding inflation, hawkish central bank expectations, and a stronger dollar against gold and Asian FX.
