> [!abstract] Overview
> Global markets start the week repricing a more hawkish Federal Reserve after Chair Kevin Warsh used Jackson Hole to signal rates could rise unless inflation improves, lifting September hike bets and pushing equities, gold, and bonds lower. That repricing collides with fresh US-Iran escalation: US forces struck Iranian rocket launchers near the Strait of Hormuz, Iran vowed retaliation, and Treasury pointed to weekly secondary sanctions, driving oil higher and widening the risk premium. Washington also targeted a Hong Kong entity and a UAE bank, drawing China and Gulf channels into the sanctions fight as G20 finance meetings open. China’s official manufacturing PMI rose 0.6 percentage points in August, offering a stabilising domestic data point. The dominant thread remains a hawkish dollar plus energy risk premium tightening financial conditions into a week of G20 diplomacy, China data, and US policy speaker risk.
