> [!abstract] Overview
> The BOJ raised its policy rate to `1.25%`, the highest since 1995, by a 7-2 vote, but Governor Ueda's openness to further tightening failed to stop the yen weakening past `157` to a two-week low while the Nikkei 225 gained `1.5%` and 10-year JGB yields slipped. The yuan was the standout in Asia, with onshore and offshore both breaking `6.7` to a four-year high as the Fed hike was read as US confidence and investors positioned for the Trump-Xi summit and broader clearing access. Oil extended its decline on signs Saudi supply disruption is easing, though Hormuz traffic stayed below the 10-day average and VLCCs continued to avoid the Red Sea, lifting refined product cracks. ECB household inflation expectations rose in August, keeping euro-area tightening pressure alive even as Vujcic pushed back against oil-fuelled rate bets. The cross-market thread is central banks hiking into geopolitical stress, with currencies pricing guidance credibility rather than rate differentials.
