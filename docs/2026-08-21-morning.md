> [!abstract] Overview
> The dominant macro driver is the US fiscal premium: long-dated Treasury prices are sliding and 30-year yields are rising even as the Treasury doubles buyback purchases, with the operation said to exceed `$4 billion`, and the stress is now spilling into the dollar as dealers turn bearish on the greenback and describe it as the fiscal pressure valve. Iran risk is re-accelerating, with Washington vowing its toughest sanctions and threatening economic action while international oil prices jump more than `2%`. The US debt stock has crossed `$40 trillion`, intensifying concern about long-end supply absorption. The cross-market thread is US fiscal credibility: failed long-end intervention is redirecting stress into the dollar and into energy risk premium.
