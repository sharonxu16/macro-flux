> [!abstract] Overview
> Semiconductor stocks cratered across Asia, led by a nearly 15% plunge in SK Hynix, as China’s reported breakthrough in immersion DUV lithography and fears of circular AI funding triggered the most aggressive rotation out of the chip trade since 2021. The rout dragged the Kospi sharply lower and pushed the KRW weaker, with U.S. equity futures pointing to a deepening tech-led sell-off at the Wall Street open. Oil extended its overnight collapse toward `$80` on Brent as a pause in U.S. strikes against Iran and nascent shipping normalization through Bab el-Mandeb continued to deflate the war premium. The cross-market thread is an accelerating unwind of the AI and Iran risk premia simultaneously, reinforcing a risk-off tilt in Asia FX and equities just 30 hours before the Fed’s rate decision.
