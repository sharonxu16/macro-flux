> [!abstract] Overview
> The dominant macro driver is a renewed repricing of US rate expectations after Fed Chair Kevin Warsh delivered a widely expected hike and signaled more tightening, sending front-end yields to multi-year highs, lifting the dollar to its best week since June, and dragging equities lower. The resulting rate differential is amplifying pressure on the yen despite the Bank of Japan raising rates to the highest since 1995, with Tokyo’s rate check only paring the decline. Oil’s supply shock from the Saudi East-West pipeline closure is being treated as less disruptive than feared, with crude finishing the week flat even as Aramco halts some European deliveries and France calls a G7 reserve meeting. US-China pre-summit friction is building after Trump signed a Russia sanctions bill with sweeping tariff powers days before Xi’s state visit. The common thread is higher-for-longer US rates tightening financial conditions while energy and trade policy keep tail risks elevated across Asia FX and commodities.
