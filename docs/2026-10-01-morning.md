> [!abstract] Overview
> The dominant macro driver remains the global real-yield shock: the 10-year Treasury yield pushed to a fresh 24-year high and the dollar posted its best month since June even though August core PCE landed below expectations, while consumer spending and ADP hiring suggested the economy is accelerating. Eurozone inflation jumped to multi-year highs, with the ECB signaling it may need pre-emptive tightening as energy costs feed into prices. Oil whipsawed as Middle East crude exports returned to prewar levels but banks still raised 2026 price forecasts and US energy executives warned diesel may not normalize for a year. China marked National Day with Xi projecting confidence and another measured stimulus drip-feed, while Korea ended Q3 as the world’s worst equity market on an AI-memory unwind. The cross-market thread is tighter global financial conditions: high real rates plus oil-driven inflation are keeping central banks hawkish and leaving Asia FX and risk assets vulnerable.
