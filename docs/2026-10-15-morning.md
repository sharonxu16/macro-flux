> [!abstract] Overview
> September US CPI accelerated to `3.7%` headline with core also beating estimates, rekindling October and December Fed hike bets and sending the dollar higher while AI-heavy equities sank. The inflation shock compounds an escalating Middle East supply threat: Houthi strikes hit Saudi Aramco’s headquarters, a container ship sank in the Strait of Hormuz, and Brent traded above `$106`. China’s September trade data reinforced its export-led reflation story as exports rose `36.3%` and CPI rose `0.9%`, but Li Qiang pledged more high-value imports amid EU calls for new trade weapons. The common thread is a hawkish policy repricing driven by war-linked energy costs and hot goods inflation, favoring a stronger dollar, higher oil, and pressure on long-duration risk assets across DM and Asia.
