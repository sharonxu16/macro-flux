> [!abstract] Overview
> The Reserve Bank of Australia delivered the session's dominant policy shock, lifting its cash rate by `25 basis points` to `4.35%`, the highest in 15 years, while warning that inflation risks are tilted upward and further tightening is not excluded. That hawkish signal lands inside a global rates repricing where traders are debating whether the Federal Reserve resumes hikes in October, with Hong Kong assets in focus and gold under renewed pressure as US real yields climb. Singapore added a targeted equity-market support measure, with MAS allocating `S$1.45 billion` to five asset managers and committing `S$20 million` to strengthen market making. The cross-market thread is tightening developed-market rate expectations compressing gold and rate-sensitive Asia assets while selected local policy supports cushion Singapore equities.
