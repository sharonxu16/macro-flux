> [!abstract] Overview
> A hawkish Fed repricing and a physical oil supply shock are driving global markets together. The 10-year Treasury yield hit `5.04%`, the highest since 2007, with traders pricing more than a 90% chance of a Fed hike on Wednesday as profit-taking and oil-driven inflation fears deepen the bond selloff. Crude rallied on Saudi cargo cancellations after a pipeline hit and further obstruction of oil routes, tightening the Strait of Hormuz physical supply picture. US equities fell, EM assets extended their slide, and risk appetite stayed defensive into the FOMC decision. The cross-market thread is an energy-driven inflation and rates shock that forces the Fed toward a hawkish hike, squeezes duration and risk assets, and keeps Asia FX on the defensive.
