> [!abstract] Overview
> The weekend’s dominant tension was a much weaker September payrolls print colliding with renewed Middle East oil-shock risk: the US economy added only `29,000` jobs, resetting Federal Reserve hike expectations, while Iran refused to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and the G7 approved a `100 million`-barrel emergency diesel and crude release. Indo-Pacific security risk accelerated as North Korea tested an intermediate-range missile and the US deployed anti-ship missiles to Yonaguni, while Beijing and Brussels traded new trade probes and tariff threats. The cross-market thread is monetary easing relief from weak US growth fighting energy-driven inflation and geopolitical risk premia, capping any durable bond rally.
