> [!abstract] Overview
> The Federal Reserve delivered its first rate increase since 2023, a unanimous 25 basis point hike with forecasts pointing to one more move this year. The dollar posted its best day in three months, the 10-year Treasury yield reclaimed the 5% mark, gold fell, and US equities dropped sharply. Hong Kong’s monetary authority matched the Fed immediately, raising its base rate for the first time in over three years and threatening the property recovery. Oil extended its decline as Saudi Arabia moved to restore a key pipeline, even while Chinese oil prices hit record highs after the earlier pipeline attacks. The cross-market thread is a more hawkish Fed and stronger dollar tightening global financial conditions, while energy and Russia sanctions risks keep inflation and geopolitics in play just before Xi’s Washington visit.

### 📌 Fed hikes first time since 2023; Warsh stresses price stability, HKMA follows

**Fact**

The Federal Reserve raised interest rates by a quarter percentage point and penciled in an additional hike later this year, steps aimed at containing inflation that will test Chairman Kevin Warsh’s relationship with President Donald Trump ([BBG 1](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-16/fed-raises-rates-as-warsh-bucks-trump-to-contain-inflation)). The Fed’s Federal Open Market Committee voted unanimously to raise rates by a quarter of a percentage point ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/united-states-canada/article/3367774/us-federal-reserve-raises-interest-rates-first-time-3-years-risking-trumps-ire)). The 10-year yield moved above the `5%` mark after a Federal Reserve rate increase and comments from Chairman Kevin Warsh highlighting persistent inflation risks ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/treasury-yield-bond-market-fed-decision.html)). The dollar notched its best day in three months on prospects that the Federal Reserve will continue to raise interest rates ([BBG 2](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-16/dollar-jumps-after-fed-raises-rates-sends-hawkish-signal)). Gold held a decline after the Federal Reserve raised interest rates for the first time in three years and signaled another hike was likely before year-end ([BBG 3](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-16/gold-holds-decline-after-fed-tilts-hawkish-and-raises-rates)). > * **Narrative change**: Reversal/Fading of the Middle East oil supply shock as pipeline restoration proceeds, though Chinese domestic draws remain tight.
> * **Transmission / Market Read**: Energy risk-premium channel favors **short Brent** and **long Asia energy importers**; record Chinese oil prices limit disinflation relief.
> * **Watchpoint / Confidence**: Confirmation of full Saudi pipeline restart and any fresh Hormuz transit disruption. Confidence: Medium.
