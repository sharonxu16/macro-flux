> The global sovereign bond selloff extended sharply intraday, with the 10-year Treasury yield hitting its highest since 2002 and UK long-term borrowing costs reaching `6%` for the first time since 1998, while the euro slid to a `17-month` low and French fiscal risks widened spreads toward crisis territory. The BOJ’s summary of opinions showed rate setters saw the case for faster hikes while Japan’s largest manufacturers turned more optimistic, keeping upward pressure on global yields. The cross-market thread remains tighter global financial conditions: high sovereign yields, an energy-driven inflation impulse, and widening policy divergence are keeping the dollar bid and risk assets vulnerable.
