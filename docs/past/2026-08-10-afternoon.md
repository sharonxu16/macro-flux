## 🔬 Narrative Watch

### 📌 Trump shifts to economic pressure on Iran; oil stabilises

**Fact**
US President Donald Trump signalled he will rely on economic pressure rather than immediate new military strikes ([Al Jazeera 1](URL)). The US is leaning on sanctions and a naval blockade as oil prices stabilise, easing the economic strain on American consumers ([Al Jazeera 2](URL)). Trump’s pivot comes as Iran hardened its stance, with Tehran saying the Strait of Hormuz will not reopen without major US concessions ([Al Jazeera 3](URL)). Oil prices were little changed as investors awaited concrete signs of a Hormuz deal ([WSJ 1](URL)), though Brent rose earlier amid growing doubts that the waterway can reopen soon ([WSJ 2](URL)). The two sides remain “far from resolving a crisis that has choked the Strait of Hormuz for more than five months” ([CNBC](URL)).

> [!info] [AI Reasoning]
> * **Narrative change**: Reversal — the White House is stepping back from the threat of military escalation, reducing the immediate war‑risk premium built into oil markets.
> * **Transmission / Market Read**: Risk‑premium channel favours **short Brent** and **long Asia FX** on easing supply‑shock fears, though the Hormuz remains blocked and Iranian demands cloud the outlook.
> * **Watchpoint / Confidence**: Any confirmed transit through the Strait of Hormuz or formal framework agreement; Confidence: Medium given the history of volatile US‑Iran messaging.

### 📌 BOJ July summary flags upside price risks, possible faster rate hikes

**Fact**
The Bank of Japan flagged rising risks of inflation heating up in a summary of opinions from its July meeting, with one board member pointing to the possibility of an acceleration in the pace of interest rate hikes ([BBG](URL)). “One rate‑setter noted that the central bank could deliver more rate rises than market expectations” ([FT](URL)). The yen fell intraday as the dollar steadied after Friday’s payrolls drop ([Reuters](URL)).

> [!info] [AI Reasoning]
> * **Narrative change**: Acceleration — the BOJ’s hawkish leaning challenges the otherwise dovish global rate repricing triggered by weak US jobs, injecting a new policy divergence into FX markets.
> * **Transmission / Market Read**: Policy‑channel favours **long JPY** on a medium‑term horizon, though the immediate reaction was yen weakness as the dollar stabilised.
> * **Watchpoint / Confidence**: Any further BOJ board commentary or a September meeting shift; Confidence: High on the hawkish signal, but the influence on spot JPY may be gradual until actual action.
