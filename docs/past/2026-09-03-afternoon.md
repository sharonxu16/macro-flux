> The dominant afternoon repricing was in Japan rates and the yen, with the BOJ reported to favor a quarter-point hike this month, the 30-year JGB auction coming in better than feared, and Fed Governor Waller’s remarks easing US rate-hike bets. The yen strengthened 2% Thursday, reversing a month of gradual decline, as traders lifted bets on Japanese interest-rate hikes and were on high alert to the risk of authorities wading back into the market to boost the currency ([BBG 2](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/yen-traders-brace-for-holiday-intervention-risk-around-boj)). ### 📌 PBOC rejects G20 trade imbalance claims and rules out yuan devaluation

**Fact**
China has fired back at critics of its trade imbalances at a Group of 20 gathering, with the central bank firmly denying that Beijing deliberately pursues trade surpluses, while also vowing that it would not weaponise the yuan to boost exports ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/economy/global-economy/article/3366213/china-rejects-g20-trade-imbalance-claims-says-no-need-devalue-yuan)). People’s Bank of China governor Pan Gongsheng said the country remained committed to expanding domestic demand ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/economy/global-economy/article/3366213/china-rejects-g20-trade-imbalance-claims-says-no-need-devalue-yuan)). “Addressing global imbalances requires all countries to promote their own structural reforms,” Pan was quoted as saying during the two-day G20 meeting of finance ministers and central bank governors, which ended on Tuesday ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/economy/global-economy/article/3366213/china-rejects-g20-trade-imbalance-claims-says-no-need-devalue-yuan)). China’s trade surplus hit a record `$1.2 trillion` last year ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/economy/global-economy/article/3366213/china-rejects-g20-trade-imbalance-claims-says-no-need-devalue-yuan)).

> [!info] [AI Reasoning]
> * **Narrative change**: Confirmation because PBOC explicitly rules out yuan devaluation and defends trade imbalances, consistent with stable RMB policy.
> * **Transmission / Market Read**: Capital-flow channel supports **CNH stability** and lessens depreciation risk, mildly positive for China rates and HK tech flows.
