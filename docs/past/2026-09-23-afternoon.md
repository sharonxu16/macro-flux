> [!abstract] Overview
> The dollar extended to two-month highs and the euro hit its lowest in nearly two months as traders added to Fed-hike bets after Boston Fed President Susan Collins warned inflation could stay notably above target, while the OECD and ADB both flagged broadening global price pressure. Brent slid below `$99` for its longest losing streak in more than 12 months on US-Iran diplomacy, but physical shipping stress intensified with supertanker rates hitting a record `$1.2mn` a day and Hormuz traffic still below the 10-day average. Singapore core inflation accelerated for a third month to its highest since 2024, and Bank Indonesia held rates while leaning on intervention. Into Thursday's Xi-Trump summit, Beijing called Taiwan and human rights red lines and Chinese business leaders looked likely to skip the trip. The cross-market thread is hawkish dollar-supporting global inflation colliding with oil-driven disinflation, leaving the USD and rates bid while crude's slide only partially cools Asia import costs.
