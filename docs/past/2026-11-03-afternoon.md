> [!abstract] Overview
> Risk assets are caught between US election-day caution and a fresh Middle East escalation, as the US struck Iran's Qeshm Island and Tehran said a war deal remains distant, lifting oil off this morning's selloff and reviving the energy risk premium. The more consequential Asia move was China's decision to raise the FX reserve requirement ratio to `6%`, the first such tightening in nearly five years, after the yuan broke `6.65`, knocking offshore yuan about `200 points` lower to cool the rally. The RBA raised its cash rate to `4.85%`, an 18-year high, but a split vote and Governor Bullock's signal of a pause argue the tightening cycle has peaked. China also reportedly ordered banks to pause IPOs while Nvidia directed data-centre clients to reduce China exposure, reinforcing a policy-heavy and decoupling session for Chinese assets. The cross-market thread is a reversal of this morning's de-escalation impulse: geopolitical risk and energy supply fears are back, while China leans against both yuan strength and equity weakness.
