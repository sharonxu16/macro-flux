> [!abstract] Overview
> Risk sentiment improved in the Asia afternoon as oil pulled back on signs that Hormuz flows are easing, while Treasury yields retreated and US equity futures advanced into the Trump-Xi summit. US-China diplomacy added momentum after Treasury Secretary Bessent described his meeting with Vice Premier He Lifeng as successful and officials discussed a new AI notification mechanism, lifting Chinese biopharma and broader equities. Korea presented a competing signal: record early September exports on the chip boom prompted a major bank to flag upside risk to its already hawkish `3.75%` BOK rate call, even as a suspected landmine blast injured South Korean soldiers in the DMZ. Iran remained a live tail risk with Trump threatening no-deal consequences and Tehran warning of retaliation "without limitations". The common thread is an afternoon de-escalation in the oil premium and a diplomatic push on US-China friction, but Korean rate-hike risk and Iran escalation warnings keep the macro backdrop fragile.
