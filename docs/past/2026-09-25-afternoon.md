> [!abstract] Overview
> The global bond rout paused this afternoon as oil pulled back from its highs, with the 10-year Treasury yield slipping to about `5.17%` after a two-day surge of more than 20 basis points, though Asian bond yields kept climbing. Iran’s offer to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days and restart nuclear talks collided with fresh Houthi missile and drone strikes on Saudi targets, leaving the de-escalation signal fragile. The dollar eased intraday versus the yen but remains on track for its best two-week stretch in six months on Fed tightening bets, while Japan signaled fresh intervention risk and BOJ price pressures supported another hike. The cross-market thread is a tentative risk respite from lower energy and stabilizing Treasuries that has not yet broken the dollar’s tightening impulse over Asia FX.
