> [!abstract] Overview
> Trump called off military strikes against Iran and announced new negotiations starting Monday, sending oil prices sharply lower and collapsing the Strait-of-Hormuz disruption premium. The US Treasury executed a historic direct intervention to buy yen alongside Japan, reinforcing coordinated Asia FX defence and igniting a yen surge that battered Japanese and Korean equities. Inside the Fed, three dissenting officials publicly demanded a rate hike at the July meeting, breaking Warsh’s consensus hold and elevating September tightening expectations. The cross‑market thread is a sudden supply‑side disinflation impulse from oil colliding with hawkish central‑bank muscle and a coordinated yen/won rally that is punishing exporter‑heavy equity indices.
