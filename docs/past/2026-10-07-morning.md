> [!abstract] Overview
> US rate pressure eased after the prior multiyear yield surge, with Treasury yields falling and the S&P 500 closing at a record as AI-led momentum absorbed the bond-market shock, though traders immediately reloaded short bets on long-dated Treasuries. In commodities, stronger Strait of Hormuz flows are colliding with renewed tanker attacks and Houthi airfield strikes, keeping crude supported and reviving supply-risk premium. The one cross-market thread is a fragile easing of rate and oil pressure that does not resolve lingering debt, fiscal, and geopolitical risk premia.

### 📌 Treasury yields turn lower but US long-end shorts rebuild

**Fact**

Traders extended their short bets against US government bonds as long-term yields hover near a 24-year high, signaling speculation the selloff is likely to continue even after a sharp slowdown in job growth pointed to cooling in the economy ([BBG 1](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-06/bonds-traders-revive-treasury-short-bets-after-fleeting-shakeout)).
