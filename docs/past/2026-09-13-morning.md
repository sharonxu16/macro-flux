> [!abstract] Overview
> The dominant macro driver is a fresh Gulf energy shock: Saudi Arabia shut its East-West Pipeline after attacks, the US says 100 commercial vessels have been redirected under the Iran blockade, and crude has round-tripped back above `$100`. The physical supply pressure is feeding directly into G7 inflation repricing, with the ECB warning the shock will last longer and markets pressing the Fed toward rate hikes. At the same time, BRICS diplomacy produced a Gulf de-escalation signal and another step toward local-currency settlement, but operational risks from Hormuz to Bab al-Mandeb keep the energy risk premium elevated. The cross-market thread is an oil-supply shock amplifying central-bank hawkishness and keeping Asia FX and global rates on repricing watch.
