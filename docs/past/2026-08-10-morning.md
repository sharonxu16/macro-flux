> [!abstract] Overview
> Iran’s demand that Washington meet fresh conditions before any Strait of Hormuz reopening shattered hopes of an imminent deal, lifting crude and dashing the tentative de‑escalation priced late last week. Chinese July CPI and PPI both undershot consensus, the factory‑gate print recording its first deceleration since the Iran war began, reinforcing the disinflation narrative and strengthening PBOC‑easing bets that weigh on CNH. The cross‑market thread is a world where war‑induced energy‑supply anxiety coexists with a softening US labour market and deepening Chinese deflation, lifting gold and weighing on Asian carry currencies while leaving oil as the bridge between geopolitical fear and softening global demand.
