> [!abstract] Overview
> Crude reversed higher and was set for weekly gains after Washington threatened an indefinite naval blockade of Iran and shipping traffic through Hormuz remained capped, reasserting the supply-risk premium that had faded in the previous session. The yen became the sharpest FX story, with the BOJ reportedly eyeing a September hike and faster tightening while intervention fizzled and dollar-yen climbed back toward 160. China’s July credit data showed a fresh record loan contraction despite a 7.4% rise in aggregate social financing, and the PBOC responded with trillion-level outright reverse repos and its first mid-month overnight reverse repo. Washington also quantified the tariff-evasion crackdown at up to $26 billion in lost revenue and announced up to 100% levies on Chinese drones. The cross-market thread is a split between escalating geopolitical and tariff supply shocks and a still-soft US rate path that keeps equities near records but leaves energy-sensitive and Asian currencies vulnerable.
