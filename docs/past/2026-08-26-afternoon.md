> [!abstract] Overview
> Crude extended its slide toward `$80` as Iran and Oman formalized a temporary Hormuz route, but US claims that oil is already moving through the strait are still not matched by tanker tracking, so the supply-risk premium is compressed rather than extinguished. The sharper repricing is in global rates: Australia's July CPI surprised to the upside, Japanese service prices accelerated, and the ECB's Schnabel reiterated that rates must rise, even as the Treasury's buyback operations collide with the Fed's inflation fight. Gold is consolidating near a three-month high while equities hold up into Nvidia and US PCE. The cross-market thread is a market torn between cheaper-energy disinflation and a global central bank repricing that is being partly suppressed by fiscal debt-management operations.
