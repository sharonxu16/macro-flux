> [!abstract] Overview
> Intraday risk tone stabilized ahead of tonight's FOMC decision. European shares snapped two days of losses, Asian stocks rose and oil pared gains as bond yields eased; gold gained over `1%` and the dollar consolidated. Hawkish expectations remain entrenched, with economists urging a hike and traders awaiting whether the Fed signals further tightening after lifting rates. The cross-market thread is a tactical pause in the global selloff into the FOMC, with central bank convergence still the dominant pressure on duration and Asia FX, partly offset by US-China trade diplomacy.
