### 📌 BOJ Holds at `1%` but Dissenting Vote and Upgraded Outlook Cement September Hike Bets

**Fact**
The Bank of Japan held its policy rate steady at `1%` on Friday and signaled the possibility of a move in September after government officials took the rare step of wading back into the currency market just hours earlier with apparent help from the US ([BBG 1](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/boj-holds-rate-steady-with-upward-revision-to-economic-outlook)). One board member dissented, calling for an immediate increase ([BBG 2](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/bank-of-japan-holds-interest-rate-steady-at-1-as-expected)). New forecasts show the central bank to be more confident about activity and inflation, with the BOJ warning that core inflation risks exceeding `2%` ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/cb149846-6032-4883-8695-685e4e8dd886), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/boj-rates-yen-intervention-inflation-japan.html)). Following the decision the yen came under immediate pressure but Governor Kazuo Ueda’s remarks at the press conference were viewed as mildly hawkish, with some strategists now expecting an earlier rate hike ([Reuters](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMivgFBVV95cUxObG50QjFDY2VPUEhRMEx0U1JQb0lobkF2V2QwRVdVMVZKR3NEMDZEbGphZmU0RURka0xtQ1dVbHd3Y01HMDlGSkVnWXBNQ1BDa0dfNk5Ed1k2Q0lCV0dKYXdVMDI5MHlRSlBZZjktbVVBZENUdjdYRy1iOXJnVXBodzNNNEtzV3BXVmdqN2pfVWVDalRvVW5Wd3JndlBsM2pMSlVtTzBySHpoQ2pSYTd4alQ0RWl6OWNLc3hIODNB), [BBG 3](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/strategists-await-ueda-guidance-after-boj-s-steady-decision)). The yen subsequently recovered ground, retracing its post‑announcement losses.

### 📌 China’s Factory PMI Shrinks for the First Time Since February, Snaps Five‑Month Expansion

**Fact**
China’s official manufacturing purchasing managers’ index fell to `49.2` in July, missing the consensus estimate of `50.1` and slipping into contraction for the first time in five months ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/plus/news/china/politics/article/3362512/china-factories-cool-cxmt-surges-pla-marks-anniversary)). The drop was driven by a fading export rush that had powered a second‑quarter rebound, with external and domestic orders both softening ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/china-pmi-factory-activity-economic-growth-exports-.html)). Construction activity also fell to its lowest level since the pandemic, reinforcing concerns over the broad‑based slowdown.

> [!info] [AI Reasoning]
> * **Narrative change**: New — the contraction is a material downside surprise that was not reflected in the morning’s state, opening the door to further PBOC easing.
> * **Transmission / Market Read**: Growth and liquidity channel — supports **short CNH**, **long USD/CNH**, and weighs on **AUD** and industrial metals.
> * **Watchpoint / Confidence**: Caixin services PMI and July trade data due next week; Confidence: High given the official release and multiple confirming sources.
