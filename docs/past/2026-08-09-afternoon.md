### 📌 Extreme Korean equity volatility subsides as leveraged trades are flushed out

**Fact**
The most extreme phase of South Korea’s stock‑market turmoil may be over after a historic selloff flushed out leveraged positions and regulatory curbs sent trading in some of the riskiest products plunging ([BBG](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-09/korea-volatility-spike-ebbs-as-leveraged-trades-are-flushed-out)). The volatility spike that had gripped Korean equities is ebbing, with trading volumes in structured products collapsing as margin calls subside.

> [!info] [AI Reasoning]
> * **Narrative change**: Deceleration — the forced deleveraging that drove extreme KRW and KOSPI moves is largely complete, reducing immediate systemic risk.
> * **Transmission / Market Read**: Stabilisation supports **short KRW** consolidation; a return to calmer markets could revive carry trades if BOK stays dovish.
> * **Watchpoint / Confidence**: KOSPI 200‑day moving average behaviour and next KRW fixing; Confidence: Medium — flush is evident but structural leverage reset may take longer.
