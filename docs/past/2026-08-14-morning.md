> US producer price growth cooled more than expected in July and jobless claims edged up to `209,000`, reinforcing market pricing that the Federal Reserve will stay on hold in September and sending the S&P 500 to a fresh record. The common thread is a market trading near-term benign US rates and softer energy just as geopolitical and trade tail risks keep accumulating.
