> Middle East escalation is the dominant macro driver: the first Riyadh air-raid alerts since the Houthi conflict reignited and a strike on Saudi Arabia’s East-West pipeline disrupted the key bypass around the Strait of Hormuz, threatening crude deliveries to Europe and tightening diesel. That shock reinforces the Federal Reserve’s unanimous quarter-point hike and inflation vigilance, while Gulf states and Iran open a diplomatic track through Doha and the UN. The cross-market thread is energy-driven inflation expectations meeting policy tightening and US-China event risk: supply shock keeps front-end yields and the dollar supported against energy-importing Asia FX until either pipeline flows normalize or a credible ceasefire materializes.
