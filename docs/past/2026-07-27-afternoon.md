> [!abstract] Overview
> Singapore surprised with a second straight monetary policy tightening, increasing the Singapore dollar’s appreciation slope to contain oil‑ and AI‑driven inflation risks, reinforcing an Asia‑wide hawkish pivot. The shock move came alongside the unexpected resignation of Bank Indonesia’s governor, which shook confidence in rupiah stability and central‑bank independence, raising broader EM policy uncertainty. Meanwhile, China’s CXMT soared 466% on its Shanghai debut, swelling to a `3.28 trillion` yuan market cap and record turnover, crystallizing a powerful rotation into homegrown tech champions and diverting massive liquidity. Red Sea shipping slowed after Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia, while Kazakh crude loadings resumed at CPC, leaving physical oil supply risks finely balanced even as US‑Iran tensions ease. The common thread is that inflation anxiety and capital‑flow volatility are forging sharply diverging monetary paths across Asia, keeping FX markets acutely sensitive to commodity prices and central‑bank credibility.
