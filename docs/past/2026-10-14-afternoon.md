> [!abstract] Overview
> Risk appetite stabilized in Asia while two reflationary data pulses from China and sustained energy-transit stress kept policy hawks on alert. Singapore’s central bank unexpectedly kept its settings unchanged after two prior tightenings, judging the global energy crunch and AI boom will cool through 2027, while third-quarter GDP came in above estimates at `5.4%`. Brent and oil product cracks stayed firm after the energy watchdog said diesel tightness could persist into 2027 and European refiner margins slumped, with Tehran reported to be boosting ship attacks and seeking Hormuz disruption. The cross-market thread is whether this Asia-driven reflation pulse and oil supply stress force a rethink of the cautious policy stance right as US CPI lands tonight.
