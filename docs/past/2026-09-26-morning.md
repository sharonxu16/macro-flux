> [!abstract] Overview
> Global markets closed the week torn between a tentative US-Iran roadmap to reopen Hormuz and a bond rout that refuses to fade. Tehran’s seven-day reopening offer knocked Brent roughly `2%` lower, yet Houthi attacks on Saudi targets and a halted Russian refinery kept physical supply risk alive. Treasury yields ended higher again, with long yields near two-decade highs as Fed chair Kevin Warsh’s framework leaves further hikes on the table and strong activity data undermines the case for relief. Equities shrugged off the pressure to cap the week higher, while the yen recovered intraday only after hedge funds had already cut bullish bets. The cross-market thread is a world paying a higher risk premium for energy and duration simultaneously, keeping inflation break-evens and Asia FX under stress.
