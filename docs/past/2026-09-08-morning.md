> [!abstract] Overview
> The dominant macro driver is a tightening of global energy and rate conditions: Brent is holding near `$100` as the Strait of Hormuz becomes the center of an escalating tanker war, with Saudi Aramco facilities hit, transits at their lowest since May, and Qatar warning of an industrial catastrophe. Simultaneously, the BOJ repricing is forcing yen to its strongest since February while UST 10Y yields press toward `5%`, extending the global duration shock. Trade fragmentation is broadening too, with Canada preparing `15%` to `50%` retaliatory tariffs against the US on Tuesday and JLR cutting `4,000` jobs as tariffs and China competition bite. China is offsetting some external risk by accelerating gold purchases to the most since 2023 and carrying AI-backed export strength into this morning's August trade data. The common thread is that energy-driven inflation risk, BOJ-led rate repricing, and tariff escalation are converging into a globally tightening liquidity and risk premium backdrop for Asia FX and equities.
