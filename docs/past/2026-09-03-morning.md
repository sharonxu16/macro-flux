> [!abstract] Overview
> Global financial conditions tightened sharply overnight as the war-driven energy shock repriced duration across markets. US 10-year borrowing costs touched their highest since 2023 and European natural gas hit a three-year high, extending a multi-decade global bond selloff. Oil steadied in Asia after President Trump played down the prospect of a prolonged Iran conflict, but Saudi Arabia reported an Iranian tanker attack killed two Filipino sailors and Iran blacklisted more Hormuz-bound ships. US ADP private payrolls rose only `38,000` in August, the smallest gain since January, yet Fed officials framed higher Treasury yields as reflecting a strong economy. The cross-market tension remains between war-driven inflation risk and softening labor data determining whether central banks can validate the global bond selloff.
