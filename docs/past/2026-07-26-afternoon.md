**Fact**

Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said he will appeal directly to President Donald Trump over new tariffs announced by Washington, describing the levies as unjustified ([BBG](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/australian-prime-minister-albanese-to-press-trump-on-new-tariffs)). India said trade negotiations with the US have helped place it in the “lower tier” of additional tariffs imposed by President Donald Trump’s administration, allowing the country to secure an advantage for key exports ([BBG](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/india-says-us-trade-talks-helped-it-secure-an-export-advantage)). The additional duties directly undercut the diplomatic assurances given by Rubio and sharpen the risk that the tariff wall widens further ahead of midterm elections ([SCMP 2](https://www.scmp.com/week-asia/economics/article/3361861/why-new-us-tariffs-threaten-trump-governments-credibility-asean), [3](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/united-states-canada/article/3361874/100-days-us-midterms-trump-faces-defeat-amid-voter-anger-over-inflation-iran-war)).

**Fact**
Iran said on Sunday it had made progress in talks with Oman regarding management of the Strait of Hormuz, which include imposing service fees — a move the US opposes — amid a pause in US air strikes ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/middle-east/article/3361888/iran-claims-progress-oman-talks-after-us-halts-air-strikes)). The talks focus on de‑escalation around the critical chokepoint, though Washington’s objection to service fees and the continued Houthi attacks on Saudi facilities cast doubt on a near‑term breakthrough ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/middle-east/article/3361888/iran-claims-progress-oman-talks-after-us-halts-air-strikes)).

> * **Watchpoint / Confidence**: Concrete Hormuz transit agreement or official US softening on fees within 48‑72h; Confidence: Medium — talks are nascent and opposed by Washington.
