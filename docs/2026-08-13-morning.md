> [!abstract] Overview
> Subdued US July CPI, with headline inflation at `3.4%` against `3.5%` in June, is the dominant macro force, pulling down near-term Federal Reserve hike odds and lifting Asian equities, gold and Treasuries. That collides with a worsening Hormuz and Red Sea supply shock: a Houthi attack killed six, Strait traffic is near three-month lows, Saudi crude is being rerouted through Egypt, and the IEA warns of a deepening 2026 supply shortfall alongside demand destruction. The cross-market thread is a two-way tug between cooler US inflation easing policy risk and Middle East energy disruption sustaining the oil risk premium and global stagflationary tail risk.
