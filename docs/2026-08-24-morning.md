> [!abstract] Overview
> Treasury Secretary Bessent’s attempt to steer the long end has backfired, accelerating a dollar-debasement trade in which long-term borrowing costs remained elevated despite announced buybacks, inflation breakevens hit multi-month highs, gold and bitcoin surged, and the dollar fell. US-Canada trade talks collapsed, putting `50%` US tariffs on some Canadian exports into effect immediately, with Canadian retaliation set for September 8. The energy complex is tightening as Middle East and Russia-Ukraine risks keep physical supply on edge, India’s Russian crude reliance hits a record, Saudi Yanbu tanker calls fall, and Moscow rejects a Black Sea shipping truce. China’s trade surplus widened against most EU members in July while Washington pushes an America-first APEC agenda ahead of Xi’s visit. The cross-market thread is fiscal dominance colliding with trade and energy supply shocks, a mix that favors real-asset and debasement beneficiaries while keeping inflation risk premia elevated and Asia FX on the defensive.
